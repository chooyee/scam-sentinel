Authorised-push-payment scam detection · proof of concept

Scam Sentinel — does this transfer fit the account's story?

In most Malaysian scams the victim authorises the payment themselves after being deceived, so device, login and location all look normal. The money lands in a mule account and is siphoned onward within seconds. This POC reads a DuitNow account's transfer history as a sequence and flags transfers that break the account's pattern and land on a receiver showing mule behaviour — measured as fan-in over 1h/24h/7d windows, onward fan-out over 1h/24h, and pass-through speed. Everything runs in your browser; no data leaves the page.

Fan-in is a count of distinct unrelated senders into the receiver over a window; the short window (1h) is the strongest live signal because a collection mule receives from many victims at once. Fan-out counts distinct first-time receivers the account pays onward; the 1h window catches burst layering (one credit split to several accounts at once) and the 24h window the daily dispersal cycle. Pass-through is the fraction sent onward within minutes. The three onward-flow shares describe how the receiver disperses: the regional layering playbook cashes out through e-wallets, crypto OTC traders and cardless ATM withdrawals, slices amounts just under transfer limits, and hops to other freshly opened mule accounts. Because the customer authorised the payment, a high score should trigger friction, not a silent block — an in-app warning, payee confirmation, a cooling-off hold, or a call. In production these receiver signals come from cross-bank intelligence (the National Fraud Portal), since one bank only sees the senders it hosts.

04

Embedding space

Each account timeline pooled to a single vector, projected to 2D with PCA. Once trained, scam timelines pull away into their own region — fraud "lighting up as an island."

legitimate scam

Model: 19 transfer features (incl. fan-in over 1h/24h/7d, fan-out over 1h/24h, pass-through, and the onward-flow shape: non-bank exits, structured slices, young receivers) → d_model 32 → 1 self-attention head → feed-forward → attention pooling → sigmoid. On clean synthetic data the classes separate sharply; real DuitNow data is far noisier and would show lower, more realistic accuracy. Production adds multi-head attention, deeper stacks, true graph features over the mule network, and self-supervised pretraining.
